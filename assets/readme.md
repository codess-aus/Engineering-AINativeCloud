## Engineering the AI-Native Cloud - Speaker Notes & Timings (20 min)

| # | Time | Slide | Speaker Notes |
|---|------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | 0:00–1:00 | **Title: Engineering the AI-Native Cloud** | "Every conference has an AI talk right now. I want to be upfront: this isn't 'we added a chatbot to our app.' This is about what happens to your job, and your company, in the next 2 to 5 years - and how we actually engineer for it, not just bolt it on." |
| 2 | 1:00–3:00 | **From cloud-native to AI-native** | "Cloud-native redefined our infrastructure - containers, microservices, elastic scale. AI-native redefines the *loop itself*: how we go from intent, to spec, to code, to verification, to delivery. Bolting a chatbot onto your existing workflow is like running Docker on a mainframe - technically possible, but it completely misses the point. This is a redesign, not a decoration." |
| 3 | 3:00–5:00 | **Rule Zero: You own the code** | Tell the anecdote: building a companion conference site via Copilot on your phone, the agent fabricated 3 case studies and statistics to "make the story more compelling." "It was my name on that Pull Request. I'm the one accountable - not the model. That's Rule Zero: you own the code. It's the single most important idea in this talk." |
| 4 | 5:00–6:30 | **Who gets replaced vs. who becomes irreplaceable** | "There are two developers in this new world. One lets the AI do the thinking, ships whatever it hands back, and becomes a bottleneck, not a multiplier - easily replaced. The other brings full expertise and judgment, and accepts accountability for the output. Judgment doesn't scale on its own - that's what makes it valuable." |
| 5 | 6:30–8:30 | **New workflows, agentic tools, and operating models - The Fleet** | "The new operating model isn't one developer plus one assistant. It's one expert directing a *fleet* of coding agents in parallel. And a pattern I love: 'Rubber Duck' - one agent's work gets reviewed by a second agent before it ever reaches a human. That's a quality gate, not just a velocity boost." |
| 6 | 8:30–10:30 | **The backlog has no bottom - until now** | "No one has ever seen the bottom of the backlog. Now a PO or PM can assign a low-risk, low-priority ticket straight to an agent, have it peer-reviewed by a second agent, and clear tickets that would've sat for years. It changes who can 'write code' - and what 'done' means for a team." |
| 7 | 10:30–11:30 | **Demo intro** | "Let me show you this for real. This is an unscripted recording: I assign a low-priority backlog issue to a coding agent, a second agent reviews the PR, and I'll narrate the loop, sped up." |
| 8 | 11:30–14:30 | **[Recorded demo] Coding agent + agent reviewer** | Narrate live over the footage: agent picks up the issue → writes code and tests → opens a PR → second agent reviews it (Rubber Duck) → you approve with a judgment call. "Notice - I checked the diff. I own this merge. That's Rule Zero, even at agent speed." |
| 9 | 14:30–16:00 | **The 2–5 year business cliff** | "Companies planning to write the same code, just faster and cheaper, will be out of business in 5 years - maybe 2. The winners aren't cutting cost. Same team, same budget - but instead of telling the business 'pick 2 of your 10 projects,' they're doing all 10." |
| 10 | 16:00–18:00 | **Reliability, security, and governance at scale** | "The innovation is happening at the frontier labs - OpenAI, Anthropic, and the Asia-Pacific labs pushing hard right now. But raw model access means *you* bolt on security, governance, compliance, privacy yourself. GitHub Copilot lets you pick any model from any lab, wrapped in one integrated control plane - governance built in, not bolted on." |
| 11 | 18:00–19:00 | **Recap: Rule Zero, scaled** | "Rule Zero doesn't disappear as you scale to fleets of agents - it gets *more* important. The expert directing 10 agents is accountable for all 10 agents' output. Judgment is the multiplier. Governance is the guardrail." |
| 12 | 19:00–20:00 | **Thank you / Q&A** | Close with contact info and book reference (Hands-On AI-Native Software Development). Open the floor: "Questions - or tell me what's stopping your team from trying this today." |
